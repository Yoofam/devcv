FAQ

Common questions

Everything you might want to know before you start.

Is DevCV really free?

Yes. There's no account, no paywall, and no watermark on your printed resume. Build and export as many versions as you need.

Where is my data stored?

Nowhere but your browser tab. DevCV doesn't send your details to a server — the preview is generated entirely on your device with JavaScript.

Can I edit my resume after previewing it?

Absolutely. Keep editing any field and click "Preview CV" again to re-render the document with your changes.

How do I get a PDF?

Click "Print / Save CV," then choose "Save as PDF" as the destination in your browser's print dialog.

Who do I contact if something's broken?
